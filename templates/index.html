// 01. ABOUT THE CHAPTER

A Campus Launchpad for Builders.

Hack with India — MCET Chapter is an autonomous student developer community at Methodist College of Engineering & Technology. We turn ideas into working software through code sprints, hackathons, and peer problem-solving.

01 /

Hands-on Code Culture

We believe students learn software engineering by building real things. From API development to hardware sensor integration, members collaborate on projects that solve practical problems.

02 /

Hackathon Infrastructure

Operating internal hackathons with physical lab desk allotments, assigned mentor reviews, transparent 100-point rubrics, and hybrid streaming for remote team members.

03 /

Peer & Alumni Guidance

Regular technical check-ins with senior student leads, MCET faculty advisors, and alumni engineers to review system architecture and prepare students for competitive engineering roles.
